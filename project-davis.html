Selected project

DAVIS Traffic Perception System

Through Clouds and Fog: an air–ground road traffic perception system powered by visible and infrared sensing and AI.

2025 National College Student Digital Media Technology Works and Creativity Competition · First Prize, National Finals

Live demo Project materials

Background

Rain, fog, and low light make road monitoring challenging. This project combines infrared and visible-light sensing with image enhancement, object detection, and multimodal fusion to identify vehicles and road conditions, presenting the results in a visual traffic monitoring system.

The system analyzes traffic incidents, vehicles, and behavior. It combines large language models with a traffic regulation knowledge base to support both perception and interpretation.

My Contributions

I designed and implemented the AI modules, with two main responsibilities:

  • Built a traffic violation recognition model to automatically detect behaviors such as running red lights, driving against traffic, and making illegal lane changes.
  • Built a knowledge base from traffic regulations and designed a question-answering module to explain violation categories, penalties, and the relevant regulations.

System Components

Visible–Infrared Fusion

Combines visible-light and thermal infrared imaging for object detection and image enhancement at night and in rain or fog.

Python · OpenCV · Visible–infrared fusion

Behavior Recognition & Q&A

Combines object detection, trajectory analysis, and language model interpretation to recognize traffic behavior and connect it with relevant regulations.

YOLO · DeepSeek · Knowledge base

Visualization

Brings perception results and analysis together in a visual interface for traffic incidents, vehicles, and behavior.

Vue.js · WebGIS · ECharts
