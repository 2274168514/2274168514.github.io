Unitree G1 Autonomous Recovery

A closed-loop system for fall detection and autonomous recovery in a bipedal robot.

Apr. 2026 — Jul. 2026 · Project lead: Guangyu Sun

Experiment Overview

All videos below were recorded in MuJoCo simulation. They show the Unitree G1 autonomously returning to a standing position from five initial poses: supine, prone, left side, right side, and seated or kneeling.

The system combines pose detection, reference trajectory scheduling, a pretrained ONNX tracker, and protective logic to control the transition from a fallen state to standing. These videos demonstrate individual recovery poses; the results apply to the simulation setup.
